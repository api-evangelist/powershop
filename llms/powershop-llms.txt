# Powershop

> Powershop is an Australian retail energy brand — electricity in New South Wales, Victoria, south-east Queensland and South Australia, gas in New South Wales and Victoria — operated by Powershop Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 41 154 914 075), a Shell Energy Australia business since February 2022. It has no developer programme, no API keys and no self-serve documentation. Its entire API surface is statutory: a verified Consumer Data Right (CDR) energy implementation. Open tariff data, closed consumer data.

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-07-27. Powershop publishes no llms.txt of its own (probed: 404 on www.powershop.com.au, public.cdr.powershop.com.au and cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au). This file is generated from the apis.yml catalog entry and the artifacts in this repository.

## What you can call right now, with no credentials

- [List Powershop energy plans](https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/powershop/cds-au/v1/energy/plans): 482 retail plans (416 electricity, 66 gas) served by the Australian Energy Regulator. Requires an `x-v: 1` header. No key, no signup.
- [Get one plan's detail](https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/powershop/cds-au/v1/energy/plans/PSH1060421MRE2%40EME): full tariff, discounts, eligibility and geography. Requires `x-v: 3`.
- [CDR status](https://public.cdr.powershop.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/status): Powershop's own machine-readable status endpoint. Requires `x-v: 1`.
- [CDR scheduled outages](https://public.cdr.powershop.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/outages): planned maintenance windows. Requires `x-v: 1`.

## What you cannot call without accreditation

Accounts, balances, invoices, billing, payment schedules, concessions, electricity service points, metering usage and DER (solar) register data. Reaching any of them requires ACCC accredited data recipient status, CDR Register-issued mutual-TLS certificates, and an explicit consumer consent. There is no key, sandbox or partner tier.

## APIs

- [Powershop CDR Generic Tariff (Energy Plans) API](https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/): the open half. Base URL `https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/powershop/cds-au/v1`.
- [Powershop CDR Discovery API](https://consumerdatastandardsaustralia.github.io/standards/#common-apis): status and outages on Powershop's own registered host. Base URL `https://public.cdr.powershop.com.au/cds-au/v1`.
- [Powershop CDR Energy Consumer Data API](https://www.powershop.com.au/privacy-policy/cdr-policy): the consented half. No anonymous base URL is published.
